HELLO!

I create portraits that feel personal.

I work primarily with charcoal, focusing on faces, texture, light and expression. My goal isn't simply to reproduce a photograph — it's to preserve the feeling behind it.

Each drawing is made slowly and by hand, turning a photograph or memory into something tangible you can keep.
